Compatibility with R metafor 5.2-1

This project provides a staged native Julia API with its own functions and result types. R metafor 5.2-1 is the comparison release; current claims apply only to the cases listed here. The full exported-name inventory and longer-term parity target are in COVERAGE.md.

CapabilityStatusCurrent native scope and limits
2×2 effect sizespartialescalc_rr, escalc_or, and escalc_rd compute log risk ratios, log odds ratios, and risk differences with their large-sample sampling variances. The four zero-cell policies (only0, all, if0all, none) are represented by to; the default is add=0.5, to=:only0. Other measures, inputs, and escalc() options are unsupported.
Univariate FE and REMLpartialfit_meta() fits matrix-design models with FE or one-component REML. BCG estimates, the zero-heterogeneity boundary, local-maximum safeguard, and single-study boundary are checked against the pinned reference. Zero sampling variances are rejected. Small-sample tests and alternative interval methods are unavailable.
Missing and invalid rowspartialMissing and non-finite rows are omitted with a Julia warning, and original row indices are retained. Warning text and categories differ from R. Zero sampling variances are rejected explicitly.
Formula and table inputpartialfit_meta_formula() uses StatsModels and Tables.jl. Numeric formula and row alignment cases are checked; full R formula semantics, every contrast, and every option are unsupported.
Mean and new-study intervalspartialpredict() returns normal intervals for supplied design rows. A new-study interval is available for supported REML fits; it is unavailable for FE fits. These are not all predict.rma() options.
Forest outputpartialforest_plot_data() returns study intervals and an optional pooled summary on the log-risk-ratio scale. It does not reproduce forest.rma() layout or graphical options.
Supplied phylogenetic correlationpartialfit_phylo() fits one species-level variance component using a supplied, labelled correlation matrix. Zero-boundary and interior synthetic cases are checked against R. If the random-effect design is contained in the fixed-effect design, REML warns and fixes the unidentifiable variance at zero. Tree estimation and unseen-species prediction are unsupported.
Dependent-effects GLS and REMLpartialfit_meta_cov() accepts a supplied sampling covariance V and supplied PSD covariance kernels. Correlated GLS, a two-component nested fit, and a two-outcome unstructured covariance fit are checked on fixtures. At most eight independent scalar components are accepted. These fixtures do not cover all rma.mv() families or options.
Nested random interceptspartialfit_meta_nested() builds indicator kernels from grouping vectors. Inner labels must include parent labels as tuples to avoid collisions. Other crossed or correlated structures are not claimed.
Two-outcome unstructured effectpartialfit_meta_un() fits a PSD 2×2 within-study covariance with supplied sampling V; the synthetic 20-study case matches the pinned R reference. Three or more outcomes and other struct values are unsupported.
Dependent-effects formula/table adapterpartialfit_meta_cov_formula() uses StatsModels and Tables.jl and retains original row indices and IDs while masking both axes of V. Numeric formula and row-order cases are checked; categorical contrast parity and full R formula semantics are unsupported.
Other model families and inferenceunsupportedGLMM, Mantel–Haenszel, Peto, location-scale, selection models, AR/CAR and spatial structures, estimated sampling correlations, profile intervals, Knapp–Hartung inference, and the broader diagnostic suite are not implemented.

Reference and dependencies

Reference values are tied to CRAN metafor 5.2-1, SHA-256 25c421cf52279294b11ab3166adfeb327dd22034a03a46487f081a19a163339b. The archive and normalized version are recorded in reference provenance. The Julia package does not call R and does not need the R package at runtime; R is used to regenerate the saved fixtures. Julia's declared dependencies include StatsModels, Tables, Optim, and Distributions; formula support uses StatsModels and table input follows Tables.jl.

The long-term goal is full parity across the public functions and documented options of the pinned R release, with native Julia documentation for each implemented function and option. The staged roadmap is inventory, KNHA small-sample inference, the actual Berkey data example, then broader families and option coverage. See COVERAGE.md.
