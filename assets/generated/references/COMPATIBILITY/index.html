Compatibility with R metafor 5.2-1

This is an experimental, staged port. Status describes only the function and options listed in each row.

Function or optionStatusCurrent scope and limits
escalc(measure="RR"), to correction optionspartialLog risk ratios and sampling variances for 2×2 counts. only0, all, if0all, and none are checked against the pinned release on hand cases. Other effect sizes and correction options are unsupported.
rma(..., method="FE")partialMatrix design, inverse-variance estimates, and normal intervals. Alternative tests and small-sample inference are unsupported.
rma(..., method="REML")partialMatrix design, complete-data BCG parity, zero-heterogeneity boundary, local-maximum safeguard, and single-study boundary. REML with zero sampling variances is unsupported.
Missing and invalid rowspartialNative missing values and non-finite rows are omitted with a Julia warning and original row indices are retained. Warning text/categories do not reproduce R exactly; zero sampling variances are rejected explicitly.
Latitude meta-regressionverifiedBCG coefficients and predictions for the supplied design rows match the pinned reference.
Tables and formula inputpartialStatsModels formulas over Tables.jl inputs with explicit row IDs and row-order checks. Full R formula semantics and all contrasts are unsupported.
Mean and new-study intervalspartialNormal intervals for explicit design rows and scalar REML variance. New-study intervals are unavailable for FE fits.
Forest plot data and SVGpartialOriginal study intervals and REML summary on the log-risk-ratio scale; forest.rma visual defaults are not reproduced.
Supplied phylogenetic correlationpartialOne species variance component for a supplied, labelled correlation matrix. Zero-boundary and interior synthetic fits are checked against R. When the random-effect design is contained in the fixed-effect design, REML warns and fixes the unidentifiable variance at zero. Tree estimation and unseen-species prediction are unsupported.
rma.mv() with supplied sampling covariance Vverified on fixtures; partial overallFull positive-definite V is accepted for GLS/REML. The correlated GLS fixture matches the pinned R coefficients, standard errors, intervals, QE, QEp, and coefficient covariance. Other model families and option combinations are not covered.
fit_meta_cov() scalar covariance kernelspartialAdds nonnegative variance components for supplied PSD kernels and evaluates zero-variance faces. The tested two-component nested fit matches the pinned R reference. At most eight independent scalar components are accepted; small-sample inference and profile intervals are unsupported.
Nested random interceptsverified on fixture; partial overallfit_meta_nested() builds indicator kernels from grouping vectors. Inner labels must include parent labels as tuples to avoid collisions. Other correlated or unverified crossed structures are not claimed.
Two-outcome struct="UN" study effectverified on fixture; partial overallfit_meta_un() fits a positive-semidefinite 2 × 2 within-study covariance with supplied sampling V; the synthetic 20-study case matches R. Three or more outcomes and other struct values are unsupported.
Dependent-effects formula/table adapterpartialfit_meta_cov_formula() uses StatsModels and Tables.jl, returning original row indices/IDs while masking both axes of V. Numeric formula and row-order cases are checked; full R formula semantics are unsupported.
Other multivariate covariance structures and inferenceunsupportedAR/CAR, spatial, GEN, estimated sampling correlations, profile intervals, small-sample tests, and the full diagnostic suite are not implemented.
Other rma.* families and diagnosticsunsupportedFull public-function coverage is staged after these dependent-effects examples.
