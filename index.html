Metafor.jl

Meta-analysis methods in native Julia, with carefully bounded comparisons to R metafor.

Metafor.jl is an experimental, independently developed port of selected methods from Wolfgang Viechtbauer's R package metafor. It is not the R package and does not reproduce its full API. The compatibility reference states what has been checked and where support remains partial or absent.

Start an analysis

Install the checkout, run a BCG example, and inspect the output in the getting-started guide.

Browse the methods

Find exported functions, result fields, and supported options in the reference, or follow one of the 21 focused tutorials.

Inspect comparisons

The R vs Julia comparison page links to the generated results for effect sizes, BCG models, dependent effects, phylogeny, and table inputs.

Project and scope

The package follows a familiar workflow: calculate study effects, fit a model, interpret uncertainty, and inspect forest-plot data. Its core uses Julia arrays and structured result types; Tables.jl and StatsModels.jl adapters are available for tabular and formula inputs.

The current examples cover log risk ratios, odds ratios and risk differences from two-by-two counts; fixed-effect and REML models; meta-regression and predictions; supplied sampling covariance; nested scalar random effects; a two-outcome model; and supplied phylogenetic correlation. The compatibility reference gives the specific limits. Passing one fixture does not establish broader compatibility.

Project-intended maintainers: Shinichi Nakagawa, Yefeng Yang, and Wolfgang Viechtbauer. This describes the intended project team and does not imply that anyone has accepted a role. Wolfgang Viechtbauer is the author of the upstream R package.
